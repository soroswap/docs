# FAQ

## What is Impermanent Loss?

Impermanent loss occurs when the price of your deposited tokens changes compared to when you deposited them. It's called "impermanent" because if you withdraw when the prices return to the original ratio, the loss disappears.

## How do I earn fees?

Liquidity providers earn a percentage of the 0.3% swap fee on every trade that occurs in their pool. Fees are collected in the traded tokens and can be claimed at any time.

## Are the contracts audited?

Yes. All Soroswap contracts undergo thorough audits by leading security firms before deployment. Audit reports are published on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/soroswap).
