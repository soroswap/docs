# Sorolunch

**Sorolunch** is the token launchpad built into the Soroswap ecosystem. It enables projects to raise capital through fair, community-driven token sales without front-running or botted allocations.

## How It Works

Sorolunch employs a bonding-curve-based auction mechanism. Participants contribute ETH (or the native chain token) to fund the project, and in return receive the new token at a pre-determined rate.

### Key Features

- **Fair Launch**: No insider allocation; all participants get equal treatment.
- **Bonding Curve**: Price increases linearly as funds are raised.
- **Automatic Liquidity**: Upon completion, remaining funds are used to seed a Sorodex liquidity pool.
- **Community Voting**: Token holders can vote on which projects get featured.

## Launching a Token

1. Project submits a proposal through the [Sorolunch dashboard](https://app.soroswap.io/sorolunch).
2. The community votes on the proposal.
3. If approved, the sale goes live for a fixed duration.
4. Contributors send ETH/native tokens to receive the new token.
5. After the sale ends, liquidity is auto-deployed to Sorodex.

## Safety

- All smart contracts are audited before listing.
- No team allocations or vesting tricks — everything is transparent on-chain.
- The contract is renounced after launch.
