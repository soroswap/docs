**What is Soroswap.Finance?**\
Soroswap.Finance is the first DEX and exchange aggregator built on Stellar, powered by smart contracts on Soroban. It is both an AMM protocol and an aggregator, allowing permissionless token swaps and liquidity provision without intermediaries.

**How do I connect my wallet?**\
Go to [app.soroswap.finance](https://app.soroswap.finance), then select and approve your Stellar/Soroban-compatible wallet (e.g., Freighter, xBull, Lobstr).
