---
title: "Soroswap Aggregator"
description: "The Soroswap Aggregator splits a single trade across several Soroban AMMs to get the best execution."
---

An aggregator routes one trade across several Soroban-based AMMs, splitting it so the user gets a better price than any single protocol would give. Soroswap's [Aggregator v3](/aggregator/technical-reference/contracts/aggregator-v3) runs typed routes across Soroswap, Aquarius, SushiSwap V3, Comet and Phoenix in one atomic transaction. The [Soroswap API](/api) picks the routes, and the contract executes them.
